# GAYDAR FM — Brand System

**One line:** a very queer SoundCloud radio with taste, opinions, and no login.
**Feeling:** club-kid glamour, editorial wit, tender at the center, sharp at the edges.
**Promise:** press play and something good happens. No account, no closet, no corporate pride float.

---

## 1. What this is (and isn't)

GAYDAR FM is a native Linux radio that pulls real, playable SoundCloud tracks, scores them with a
recommender we call **the Gaydar**, and lets the listener move four dials: **Camp**, **Club**,
**Soft**, **Chaos**. It is a friend with the aux, not a platform.

It is **not**:
- a diversity-and-inclusion campaign;
- a rainbow-washed skin over a generic player;
- a museum of gay history (though it respects the elders);
- a joke account. The jokes are load-bearing, not the whole building.

---

## 2. Voice principles

1. **Specific beats sanctimonious.** Name the scene, the reference, the BPM. "Ballroom vogue at
   2am" lands; "celebrate queer excellence" evaporates.
2. **Warm at the centre, sharp at the edges.** Playful, never mean. Punch at power, algorithms,
   labels, and the listener's own taste — never at bodies, ages, or identities.
3. **Camp is a lens, not a costume.** Camp is exaggeration plus sincerity, not random capital
   letters. If a line is only a meme, it is not finished.
4. **The listener is the main character; the app is the friend with the aux.** Second person,
   present tense, short sentences.
5. **If it could appear in a bank ad, cut it.** No "diversity", "inclusion", "empowerment",
   "vibrant community", "everyone is welcome" as a substitute for an opinion.

### Words we use
queer, gay, lesbian, sapphic, trans, non-binary, butch, femme, stud, ballroom, house, kiki, drag,
camp, diva, realness, the girls, chosen family. "Dyke" appears only inside quoted scene names,
never as a punchline.

### Words we do not use
slurs against people (including "faggot", "tranny", "dyke" as an insult), "lifestyle", "preference",
"biological", "traps", "shemale", "clean" (as a sexual-health term), "gym body", "guilty pleasure"
(taste is not a crime), and any line that makes the listener the punchline.

## 3. Naming

| Name | What it means | How to write it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GAYDAR FM** | The product. | All caps in the wordmark; "Gaydar FM" in running prose. |
| **the Gaydar** | The recommender itself. | Capital G. "The Gaydar is reading." "The Gaydar took notes." |
| **the Gaydar algorithm** | Only in settings, docs, and about. | Never in hero copy. |
| **the Agenda** | The editorial layer: the curated queue, the seeded scenes, the reason a track is here. | Capital A. "On the Agenda" = up next. "The Agenda has opinions." |
| **the dials** | Camp, Club, Soft, Chaos. | Capitalised as proper nouns. Values are 0.00–1.00 in UI. |
| **signal** | Connection, stream health, client-ID status. | "Signal restored." "No signal." |
| **reading** | The Gaydar's taste model / what it currently believes about you. | "The Gaydar is reading." "A reading, not a verdict." |
| **the floor** | Home / radio. The place you dance. | "Take the floor." |
| **the booth** | Search and discovery. | "Back to the booth." |

"Naming a thing after a song" is encouraged in copy ("Sylvester disco" as a label), but never
invent a fake artist or fake scene.

## 4. Five microcopy sets

### 4.1 Empty states
- Search: "Nothing yet. The girls are typing."
- Likes: "No likes yet. Your heart is a closed venue."
- History: "No history. You blacked out. Respect."
- Radio: "The DJ is in the bathroom. Give her a minute."

### 4.2 Loading
- "Reading the room..."
- "Tuning the gaydar..."
- "Consulting the Agenda..."
- "Buffering, but make it fashion..."

### 4.3 Errors
- No network: "No signal. The gaydar needs Wi-Fi."
- Stream dead: "Stream error. That one ghosted us; skipping."
- DRM/label block: "Track unavailable. The label said no."
- Client-ID refresh: "Refreshing the client ID. SoundCloud moved the keys again."
- ALSA busy: "Device busy. Something else is hogging the aux."

### 4.4 Success
- Liked: "Liked. The girls are kicking their feet."
- Unliked: "Unliked. It's not you, it's the last track."
- Copied link: "Link copied. Send it to the group chat."
- Dial change: "Camp 0.9. The drama has been noted."

### 4.5 Settings / ALSA
- "Output"
- "Device"
- "System default"
- "ALSA talks to the hardware directly. PulseAudio and PipeWire usually show up as the default device."
- "Test tone — testing, one, two, camp."

## 5. Being very gay without caricature

1. **Specific, not squeaky.** Reference real scenes, real records, real lineages: ballroom houses,
   Chicago house, PC Music, Rio baile funk, Glasgow trance-pop. Specificity is the rainbow.
2. **No identity as punchline.** The joke is never "she's trans" or "they're non-binary". The joke
   is the algorithm, the label, the 4am decision, the listener's ex.
3. **Camp needs sincerity underneath.** Read the line in a deadpan. If it only works shouted, it is
   not camp; it is noise.
4. **Desire can be tender.** Flirt with the listener, do not grope the interface. No sex acts in
   toasts, no porn-adjacent thumbnails, no "poppers" jokes.
5. **Honour the elders.** Sylvester, Beverly Glenn-Copeland, Vjuan Allure, Kevin Aviance and
   Patrick Cowley are named with respect, not as nostalgia set dressing. Trans women and drag
   artists are authors, not aesthetics.
6. **Leave room for softness.** Not every screen needs a wink. The Soft dial must actually sound
   soft, and the copy must let it.
7. **Never punch down.** No jokes about bodies, disability, poverty, sobriety, HIV status, sex work,
   or someone's first Pride. Punch at power.
8. **Reclaim with care.** "Queer", "camp", "butch", "femme", "kiki" are ours. Slurs used against
   people are not.

## 6. Accessibility notes

- **Contrast:** body text at least 4.5:1 against the background. Neon pink is for accents, not
  paragraphs; use the cream/white token for text.
- **Do not rely on colour alone.** Dials need labels, values, and a one-word descriptor:
  "Camp, 0.82 of 1.00 — matinee diva".
- **Screen readers:** every icon-only control has an accessible name ("Like track", "Skip track",
  "Open on SoundCloud"). Heart state is announced as "Liked" / "Not liked".
- **Motion:** no strobing above 3 flashes per second. Respect "reduce motion" — swap glitter
  animations for a static gradient. Pause any marquee on hover/focus, and never hide essential
  information in it.
- **Type:** badges are uppercase and short (≤ 2 words); body copy is sentence case. Avoid long
  all-caps strings. Letter-space uppercase badges.
- **Keyboard:** dials are sliders, operable with arrows and Home/End; volume too. Focus rings must
  be visible at 2px minimum.
- **Copy length:** buttons ≤ 24 characters, toasts ≤ 80, hero headline ≤ 60. If it does not fit,
  cut a joke, not the meaning.
- **Audio:** show the real ALSA device name, keep volume 0–100 with a mute toggle, and never
  surprise the listener with a test tone at full volume.
- **Language:** English-only for now. Avoid puns that depend on English spelling in strings that
  may be localised later; keep them in copy, not in code.

## 7. Seed data notes (for developers)

- `assets/seeds.json` was validated against `api-v2.soundcloud.com/search/tracks` on
  2026-11-08 with the project client ID. All 40 queries returned at least 18 streamable results
  (most 40+), and every query cleared a playable-yield check for `mp3_*` transcodings.
- **DRM reality:** with this client ID, most tracks expose only FairPlay/Widevine HLS
  (`aac_160k`, `aac_96k`, `abr_sq`). Only tracks with an `mp3_*` transcoding play without DRM.
  The engine must filter on `media.transcodings[].preset.startsWith("mp3")` before queueing, or
  the audio layer will receive unplayable streams.
- `seed_queries` is the playable search layer: every query was checked for mp3-transcoding yield.
  `artist_seeds` is the cultural layer: some label-backed catalogs are DRM-locked, so use artist
  names for taste/tag expansion and for display, and let the query layer carry playback.
- Labels are UI-ready; `why` strings are written to be shown under an artist, not stored as data.
- Do not add a query that has not been checked for both streamable and playable results.
